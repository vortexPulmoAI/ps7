3. Preprocessing & Spatial Normalization (Implemented in src/preprocessing.py)

1

Coordinate Affine Transformation Matrix (T_world): Map discrete array indices (i, j, k) to physical Patient Coordinates (X, Y, Z) in millimeters using ImagePositionPatient and ImageOrientationPatient cosines.

T_world = [ [R11 * sx, R12 * sy, R13 * sz, Tx], [R21 * sx, R22 * sy, R23 * sz, Ty], [R31 * sx, R32 * sy, R33 * sz, Tz], [0, 0, 0, 1 ] ]
2

Isotropic Resampling: Resample continuous attenuation volumes to 1.0 × 1.0 × 1.0 mm³ using 3rd-order spline interpolation. Use nearest-neighbor interpolation for binary masks to prevent boundary contamination.

3

Radiometric Windowing (HU):
• Lung Screening Window: [-1000 HU, +400 HU] (or WL: -600, WW: 1500 -> [-1350, +150 HU])
• Soft Tissue / Mediastinal: [-160 HU, +240 HU] (WL: +40, WW: 400 HU)
Scale linearly to [0.0, 1.0] or standardize via z-score.

4

Parenchyma Parcellation: Mask out extrathoracic structures using TotalSegmentator or LUNA16 precomputed lung masks (load_mhd_mask()).

4. Stage 1 — 3D Candidate Detection & FPR (Wrapped in src/detection.py)

Engineering Note: src/detection.py provides the data prep, bounding-box coordinate transformations, dual-scale patch extraction, and FROC/CPM metric calculation. Model training on GPU runs subsequently.

1

nnDetection Data Prep: Convert annotations.csv (world coords + diameter) into 3D bounding boxes [xmin, ymin, zmin, xmax, ymax, zmax] and compile dataset.json.

2

Retina U-Net Detector: Train 3D FPN with anisotropic ResNet backbone using Focal Loss (γ = 2.0) across LUNA16's 10-fold split.

3

False-Positive Reduction (FPR): Extract dual-scale 3D patches centered on candidates:
• Internal patch (32³ mm): margin sharpness and internal attenuation
• Contextual patch (64³ mm): relation to bronchi, vessels, and pleura
Train a 3D DenseNet with Online Hard Negative Mining (OHEM) to reduce false alarms to <1.0 FP/scan at >95% sensitivity.

4

FROC / CPM Evaluation: Calculate Competitive Performance Metric (CPM) = average sensitivity at {⅛, ¼, ½, 1, 2, 4, 8} FP/scan. Target CPM ≥ 0.90.

5. Stage 2 — Volumetric Segmentation (Wrapped in src/segmentation.py)

1

Rasterize Radiologist Contours: Parse polygon edge maps (xCoord, yCoord across z-slices) from dataset/LIDC-XML-only and rasterize into 3D binary ground-truth masks.

2

3D nnU-Net v2 Dataset Compilation: Structure sub-volume crops into Dataset101_LIDC_Nodules with imagesTr and labelsTr.

3

Segmentation Loss: Compound Soft Dice + Binary Cross-Entropy (BCE) Loss.

4

Spatial Metrics: Evaluate on test splits using Dice Similarity Coefficient (DSC target: 0.82–0.86) and 95th-Percentile Hausdorff Distance (HD95) in mm.

6. Stage 3 — Morphology & Density Feature Extraction (Implemented in src/morphology.py & src/density.py)

1

Physical Volumetry: V = (Σ active mask voxels) × (sx · sy · sz) mm³.

2

Second-Moment Inertia Tensor: Diagonalize covariance tensor across mask voxels to extract orthogonal principal axes (λ1 ≥ λ2 ≥ λ3). Project onto maximal transverse slice to determine long-axis diameter (d_long), short-axis diameter (d_short), and mean diameter d_mean = (d_long + d_short) / 2.

3

3D Sphericity (Ψ): Marching cubes isosurface triangulation to measure true surface area A_surface:

Ψ = (π^(1/3) * (6 * V)^(2/3)) / A_surface
Smooth granulomas approach Ψ → 1.0; invasive lesions exhibit lower sphericity.

4

Spiculation Index: Normalized radial distance variance (σ_R² / μ_R²) and mesh boundary curvature.

5

4-Tier Attenuation Decomposition:
• Air / Entrapment: < -750 HU
• Ground-Glass Opacity (GGO): [-750, -300 HU]
• Solid Component: > -300 HU (Solid core: ≥ -160 HU)
• Macrocalcification: > +200 HU
Classify subtype: Pure GGN (<10% solid), Part-Solid (10–80% solid), Solid (>80% solid), or Calcified.

7. Stage 4 — Malignancy Risk Classification (Ground Truth Labeling)

LIDC-IDRI Ground Truth Label Scheme (Option B Benchmark Standard)

  • Malignancy rating rated independently by up to 4 radiologists on 1–5 scale.
  • Label 0 (Benign): Mean radiologist score 1.0 – 2.0.
  • Label 1 (Malignant): Mean radiologist score 4.0 – 5.0.
  • Drop Score = 3 (Indeterminate): Excluded from training set (reflects genuine clinical ambiguity, standard in literature).
  • Auxiliary Radiologist Semantics: 8 semantic attributes (subtlety, calcification, sphericity, margin, lobulation, spiculation, texture) used as auxiliary tabular features alongside IBSI PyRadiomics.

8. Probability Calibration & Clinical Triad Reporting Format

Why Raw Classifier Output Fails in Clinic

Standard tree-based classifiers (XGBoost/LightGBM) optimize ranking (AUROC), but their raw predict_proba() output is heavily miscalibrated. A raw score of 0.70 does not mean 70% of such lesions are malignant. Post-hoc calibration is mandatory:

  • Platt Scaling (Sigmoid): Fits a logistic regression transform over classifier margins. Optimal for small/moderate validation sets ($N < 1000$).
  • Isotonic Regression: Non-parametric monotonic stepwise fit. Best for large cohorts ($N > 1000$).
  • Use sklearn.calibration.CalibratedClassifierCV(method='sigmoid', cv=5) and evaluate with Brier score:
    Brier = (1/N) * Σ (p_i - y_i)²

The Full Brock (PanCan / McWilliams 2013) Mathematical Formulation

Screening reference multivariable fractional polynomial logistic regression model:

p_brock = 1 / (1 + exp(-X)) X = -6.7892 + 0.0287 * (Age - 62) + 0.6011 * I_Female + 0.2961 * I_FamilyHistory + 0.2953 * I_Emphysema - 5.3854 * [ (d_max / 10)^(-0.5) - 1.58113883 ] + beta_attenuation + 0.6581 * I_UpperLobe - 0.0824 * (NoduleCount - 4) + 0.7729 * I_Spiculation Weights (beta_attenuation): Solid = 0.0, Part-Solid = +0.3770, Pure GGN = -0.1276

Clinical Threshold: British Thoracic Society (BTS) recommends Brock score ≥ 10% be directed to FDG-PET/CT or biopsy; <10% remains under surveillance.
