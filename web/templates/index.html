Industry Standard Image Formats

In clinical radiology, DICOM (Digital Imaging and Communications in Medicine) is the universal hospital gold standard. It preserves calibrated Hounsfield Units (HU), sub-millimeter slice thickness, and 3D affine orientation matrices. In AI research, NIfTI (.nii) and MetaImage (.mhd) are the standard volumetric formats.
